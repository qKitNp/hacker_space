# Privacy

Hacker Space is designed to collect as little as possible.

## Short version

- **No accounts**, no email, no analytics, no ads  
- Bookmarks, notes, and settings stay **on your device** (`localStorage`)  
- The extension only makes read-only requests to:
  - `https://hn.tinkerers.space` — story summaries  
  - `https://hacker-news.firebaseio.com` — public HN scores
